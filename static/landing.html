The product

Finance operations run on inboxes and spreadsheets.
That does not scale, or stay safe.

Accounts payable teams manually read each invoice, look up the vendor, check budgets, hunt for duplicates and chase approvers. It is slow, error-prone and an easy target for fraud, and now for AI-targeted prompt-injection attacks.

~20 min

per invoice, by hand

Reading, keying data, vendor lookups, budget checks and approval chasing.

1-3%

duplicate or erroneous payments

Industry rule of thumb for AP leakage when controls rely on people noticing.

0 audit

trail you can trust

Logs that anyone with database access can quietly edit after the fact.

The answer

A team of AI agents with guardrails that are proven, not promised.

Flowforge coordinates specialised agents across the whole procure-to-pay workflow. Every decision is explained, every policy is checked by a model checker, and every step is written to a hash-chained ledger.

How it works

From messy text to a defensible decision

Five agents collaborate on every request. The AI can only ever make a decision stricter, never looser.

📥

Intake

Reads invoices, emails and POs. Extracts vendor, amount, department.

🛑

Guard

Treats text as untrusted. Blocks prompt-injection attempts.

🔎

Risk

Vendor list, limits, duplicates and department budget.

⚖️

Policy

Verified rules decide approve, reject or escalate to a role.

🤖

AI review

An independent LLM cross-checks the outcome before humans act.

Submit a request

Paste text or batch many invoices. No templates, plain emails work.

Agents process it live

Watch each stage run and see the reasoning in the request timeline.

Humans approve what matters

Small, clean spend auto-approves. Everything else lands in a role-based queue (Manager, Director, VP Finance).

Improve policy safely

Edit rules in Policy Studio, let the verifier find loopholes, and let AI propose a fix that is re-verified before it goes live.

Features

Built like a platform, not a prototype

Everything a finance team needs to run the workflow day to day.

📊

Live dashboard

KPIs, budget burn per department, risk signals and trends.

🛡

Policy Studio

Model-checks rules against 7 safety properties with counterexamples.

✨

AI policy repair

LLM proposes a fix, the verifier proves it, a symbolic fallback guarantees it.

🔗

Audit ledger

SHA-256 hash chain. Any edit breaks the chain and is detected.

✅

Role-based approvals

Manager < Director < VP Finance with enforced authority.

🏢

Vendor management

Allow-list, limits, blocks and computed risk scores.

🤖

Finance Copilot

Ask questions about live spend, risk and approvals in plain English.

📦

Batch and export

Process many invoices at once and export results as CSV.
